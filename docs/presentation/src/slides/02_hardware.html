ROBOFACTORY / CENTRAL HUB

RoboFactory Central Hub

Physical separation of real-time control loops and high-level autonomous intelligence via isolated USB CDC channels.

Why this setup?
Keeps real-time motor control and sensor loops isolated from higher-level AI. If the AI crashes, wheels and safety loops keep running.
